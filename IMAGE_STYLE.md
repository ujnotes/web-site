# Ujnotes Article Image Style

This guide defines the visual language for Ujnotes article images.

An article image should express the central idea in one clear visual metaphor. It should create curiosity without trying to summarise every section of the article.

## Core style

- Use **cinematic editorial photography** or highly realistic photographic illustration.
- Prefer a quiet, contemplative image over a decorative or explanatory scene.
- Build the composition around **one primary subject or one symbolic interaction**.
- Use natural materials, believable scale and realistic texture.
- Keep the result timeless. Avoid a glossy advertising finish, fantasy spectacle or generic AI concept-art polish.

## Composition

- Use a wide **16:9 landscape frame** suitable for an article header.
- Keep one unmistakable focal point.
- Leave generous negative space around the subject.
- Use simple geometry, strong silhouettes and clear tonal separation.
- Prefer an eye-level or slightly low viewpoint unless the idea requires another angle.
- The image must remain legible as a small thumbnail.

## Light and colour

- Use directional natural light, window light or a restrained shaft of light when it supports the idea.
- Allow deep shadow, but preserve meaningful detail in the focal subject.
- Prefer muted earth colours, charcoal, warm stone, subdued blue and restrained gold.
- Use colour contrast sparingly. One warm-versus-cool relationship is usually enough.
- Avoid oversaturation, rainbow palettes and uniform high-key brightness.

## Symbolism

- Translate the article into a single ordinary, tangible metaphor.
- Let the viewer discover the meaning; do not make the image a literal diagram.
- Human figures may be used when solitude, inquiry, scale or moral choice is central, but keep identity anonymous and poses natural.
- Cultural or religious objects may appear only when directly relevant and represented respectfully.

## Restraint rules

- One hero subject; at most one supporting element.
- No collections of symbolic props.
- No repeated motifs used merely to fill the frame.
- No text, labels, diagrams, logos, borders or watermark.
- No visual collage, split-screen composition or infographic treatment.
- No unnecessary particles, magical glow, cosmic effects or floating objects.
- No busy background competing with the focal subject.
- Do not combine several article themes in one image.

## Quality bar

- Materials and lighting should look physically plausible.
- Edges, reflections and shadows should be coherent.
- Avoid plastic surfaces, excessive sharpness, painterly noise and obvious generation artefacts.
- The final image should feel considered, calm and intellectually serious.

## Prompt template

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: Ujnotes article header
Primary request: Express <article idea> through one clear visual metaphor.
Subject: <one primary subject>; optional <one supporting element>.
Style/medium: cinematic editorial photography; realistic materials and natural texture.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9; one focal point; generous negative space; thumbnail-readable.
Lighting/mood: directional natural light; contemplative; restrained contrast.
Colour palette: muted earth tones with one controlled warm/cool contrast.
Constraints: no text, logos, watermark, collage, decorative props or busy background.
Avoid: painterly noise, fantasy effects, multiple competing symbols and generic AI polish.
```
